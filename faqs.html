Frequently Asked Questions

Wondering how it works? Start here.

1. What age/grade levels do you tutor?

We currently support students from grade 6–12.

2. Do you only tutor IB students?

Our main focus is IB MYP and DP students. However, some services may also be suitable for students outside the IB depending on the subject and their goals.

3. Are sessions one-to-one or group sessions?

Our tutoring is primarily one-to-one, allowing sessions to be tailored to each student's needs and pace. Based on availability, group sessions are offered to tutees who wish to use this service.

4. How long is a tutoring session?

Sessions are typically 60 minutes, depending on the student's needs and service selected.

5. How often should I have tutoring?

There is no universal answer. Some students benefit from weekly sessions, while others may need more intensive support before exams. We'll recommend a schedule based on your goals, workload, and available time.

6. Can I book a single session?

Yes. You may also book a free 20-minute session to receive a consultation and experience our services before joining Aniras Hub.

7. Can I have regular weekly sessions?

Yes. Regular sessions can be arranged for students who want ongoing support and progress tracking. You may contact us to find out about available packages and discounts.

8. What is expected from the students?

Students are expected to have access to Google Meet and be on time for classes.

To ensure efficient use of time and high-quality tutoring sessions, students must email the tutor at least 2 hours before their class to communicate the topics or questions they need assistance with during the session.

9. How much does tutoring cost?

Depending on the subject and grade level, some services are free and some are paid. To find more details, you may click here.

10. What payment methods does Aniras Hub use?

PayPal and e-transfer.

11. How can students/parents track progress?

You can receive periodic progress updates covering topics studied, areas of improvement, and recommended next steps.

12. How do I book a session?

Fill out this form.

13. What happens if I need to cancel?

Let us know at least 6 hours before the session by emailing us at AnirasHub@gmail.com and making the necessary changes on the calendar.

14. Still have a question?

We're happy to help. Contact us either at AnirasHub@gmail.com, or Aniras_Hub.
