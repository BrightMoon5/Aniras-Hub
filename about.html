THE PERSON BEHIND ANIRAS HUB

Meet the Founder

Hey there! I'm Sarina.

An IB graduate of May 2026 who helps other learners flourish.

Starting my IB journey in Grade 8, in a new language and over 7,000 km away from home, I learned that working smart and hard can take you far—but growth also asks for curiosity, resilience, and the courage to try again.

With results of 55/56 in MYP and 44/45 in the IBDP, I know the academic side of the journey. But the lessons that shaped me most came from beyond the classroom, too.

THE JOURNEY BEHIND THE RESULTS

Success is a process—not just an outcome.

My journey in gymnastics, taekwondo, and running taught me to face challenges, learn from failures, and keep working toward improvement. Sport helped me build discipline and resilience—skills that proved just as valuable in my academic life.

Service taught me something equally important: individual success means more when it creates a positive impact on other people. Through service projects that led to awards for excellence and leadership, I came to appreciate how much we can learn by helping others grow.

That's why I created Aniras Hub: to share what I've learned, support students through their own challenges, and help them become more confident, capable learners. I don't expect every student to follow the same path. I want to help each student find a path that works for them.

“Everything is POSSIBLE—and what matters more than the outcome is the PROCESS.”

One step at a time, until we celebrate your countless achievements together!
